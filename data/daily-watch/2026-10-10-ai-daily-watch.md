### 5. 🛠️ New AI Models & Tools Worth Learning
* **FLUX.3 Image (Black Forest Labs):**
  * *What it does:* State-of-the-art visual generation with 10-image multi-reference consistency, bounding-box layout prompting, and 4K output.
  * *Cost:* Free open-weights available for local testing; affordable API credits via OpenRouter/Replicate.
  * *Worth your time this week?* **Yes**, especially for generating consistent UI mockup assets and product visuals for student or client projects.
  * [Black Forest Labs](https://bfl.ai)
* **Higgsfield Katana (MCP Video Suite):**
  * *What it does:* Connects video motion graphics and video generation directly into Claude via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).
  * *Cost:* Freemium creator tier.
  * *Worth your time this week?* **Moderate**; excellent for quick social/marketing demos, but lower priority than coding agents.
  * [Higgsfield AI](https://higgsfield.ai)
* **Grok Routing Engine (xAI):**
  * *What it does:* Grok now acts as an intelligent router that dispatches requests to top specialized models (including Claude Opus and Grok live search) with Slack and Workspace connectors.
  * *Cost:* Included with X Premium / xAI API.
  * *Worth your time this week?* **Yes**, specifically for live market intelligence and web validation.
  * [xAI Grok](https://x.ai)
